Methodology

How to read the dashboard

Credit spreads

Option-adjusted spread (OAS) measures the additional yield investors demand to hold corporate credit relative to the Treasury curve, after adjusting for embedded options. Wider spreads generally indicate tighter credit conditions.

Refinancing estimates

The refinancing analysis compares the coupon on maturing Treasury securities with an estimated replacement yield from the current Treasury curve. It is a scenario estimate, not an official Treasury forecast.
